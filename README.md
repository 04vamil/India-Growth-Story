# India's Growth Story: Economic Growth, Living Standards & Youth Employment

## Overview
Economic growth is often used to measure a country's progress, but does a growing economy necessarily mean better living standards and employment opportunities?

This project explores the relationship between India's economic growth, human development, and youth employment through data analysis and visualisation. It aims to look beyond headline growth figures and understand what economic progress reveals about people's lives.

## What This Project Examines
- India's economic growth over time
- Trends in human development and living standards
- Youth employment and economic opportunities
- The relationship between economic growth and broader development

## Methodology
The project uses secondary data, Excel-based visualisations, and comparative analysis to identify trends and explore relationships between selected economic and social indicators.

## Data Sources
Data will be drawn from credible sources such as the World Bank, the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), and official Indian government publications. Specific datasets and references will be documented alongside the analysis.

## Key Findings
Evidence-based findings and interpretations will be added as the analysis progresses.

## Limitations
Differences in data definitions, coverage, and reporting periods may affect comparisons. The analysis explores relationships rather than assuming causation, and national-level indicators may not capture regional inequalities.
